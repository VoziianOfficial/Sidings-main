Overview

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how treats information that visitors choose to share through this website. It is written for a simple, static service website focused on siding installation, repair, planning guidance and enquiries.

By using this website or submitting the contact form, you acknowledge that your information may be handled as described here.

Collection

Information We Collect

We collect limited information that helps us operate the site, understand enquiries and respond to visitors. The exact information depends on how you use the website.

  • Details you intentionally enter into a contact form.
  • Basic technical information made available by your browser or hosting environment.
  • Cookie or browser-storage choices connected with the site's consent notice.

Information You Provide

When you submit an enquiry, we may receive your name, email address, message and any project details you include. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information through the contact form.

We use the email address you provide so we can answer your request and continue relevant project communication.

Important: contact form messages should be practical project enquiries, not urgent safety notices or highly sensitive personal records.

Automatically Collected Information

Like most websites, this site may generate basic technical records when pages are loaded. These can include browser type, device information, requested pages, referral details, approximate time of access and IP-related delivery information.

These records are generally used for site delivery, security, troubleshooting and understanding whether pages are working as expected.

Use

How We Use Information

Information is used in a limited, practical way: to respond to enquiries, maintain website functionality, improve page clarity and keep reasonable records of communication.

  • Replying to questions about siding installation, repair or materials.
  • Following up when you request contact through the website.
  • Protecting the website from misuse, spam or technical issues.
  • Reviewing general site performance without selling personal information.

Contact Form Data

Contact form submissions are used to understand the request and reply to the person who submitted it. If a form is unavailable or you prefer direct communication, you can contact us at .

Messages may be retained as part of ordinary business correspondence, especially when they relate to an ongoing enquiry.

Cookies & Similar Technologies

The website includes a consent notice that may store your acceptance or decline choice in browser storage so the notice does not need to appear repeatedly. The site is not written to depend on advertising cookies.

For more detail, see the Cookie Notice.

Sharing

Third-Party Services

The website may rely on ordinary third-party infrastructure such as hosting, browser font delivery, email handling or security tools. These services may process limited technical information as needed to provide their function.

We do not claim that advertising or analytics platforms are used unless they are actually present in the project.

Data Retention

We keep submitted information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose it was provided, including replying to an enquiry, maintaining records and addressing site operation needs.

Browser consent choices may remain on your device until you clear site data or the storage expires through your browser behavior.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to keep submitted information from being misused or accessed inappropriately. No website transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Control

Your Choices

You can choose not to submit the contact form, limit the information included in a message or contact us directly by email. You can also manage cookies and local browser storage through your browser settings.

Your Choices: you control what you send through the form and can clear cookie preferences from your browser at any time.

External Links

This website may link to pages or resources outside of . We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or availability of third-party websites.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for general audiences and project enquiries. It is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the website, contact workflows or legal expectations change. The date at the top of the page indicates the latest posted version.
